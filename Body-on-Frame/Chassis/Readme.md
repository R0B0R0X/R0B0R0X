We are using PVC sheet for handmade mainframe. Here are the reasons of using PVC sheets rather than a 3D printed one:

★PVC sheets are harder than plastics.

★It has a bunch of opportunities to shape it in our own way.

★Modifications can be made easily at any time.

★We waterproofed the frame with a glue coating.

★The frame is painted black for a sleek appearance.
